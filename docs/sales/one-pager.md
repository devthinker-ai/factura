# Factura — one-pagers

## Elevator (20 seconds)

Factura is the self-hosted box that makes a German business e-invoice-compliant: validate what arrives, prove the archive, issue what must go out — without paying per invoice to a SaaS or moving your books.

---

## A — SMB (self-hosted)

**Headline:** E-invoice compliance on your server

Receive, validate, archive, and issue **XRechnung 3.0.2** and **ZUGFeRD / Factur-X** without a per-invoice cloud subscription. Plain-language valid/invalid reports in German and English (with rule IDs). Tamper-evident GoBD-style archive. Optional Peppol send/receive behind your Access Point. One binary or one Docker container. Offline license — the box never phones home.

**CTA:** Book a 30-minute sandbox with your real supplier invoice.

---

## B — Steuerberater / Kanzlei

**Headline:** Give every client a defensible e-invoice layer — without becoming their ERP

Factura validates incoming EN 16931 / XRechnung documents with citeable rule IDs, archives originals with hash-chain evidence, and issues compliant outbound files. Roles (admin / editor / viewer) and TOTP MFA for the practice. Built for advisors who recommend tools their clients actually keep.

**CTA:** Design-partner pilot — a handful of live clients and a shared roadmap for multi-client Kanzlei mode.

---

## C — Embed / ISV (DACH vendors)

**Headline:** Ship EU e-invoicing in weeks, not quarters

Embed Factura behind your product with API keys, deterministic metering, and Go/JS SDKs. Ingest, validate, generate, Peppol — self-hosted next to your stack. You keep the brand; we keep the compliance engine current.

**CTA:** Two-week integration path — send us your invoice sample set.
